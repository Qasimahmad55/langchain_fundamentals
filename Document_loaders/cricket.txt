Under the sun, the cricket ground glows,
The crowd gets louder as the tension grows.
The bat meets the ball with a powerful sound,
And dreams of victory echo around.

The bowler runs in with fire in his eyes,
The batsman stands ready, watching the skies.
A six flies high, the whole crowd cheers,
A moment of glory that conquers all fears.

From dusty streets to stadium lights,
Cricket brings hearts together on countless nights.
Win or lose, we play with pride,
For cricket is passion we carry inside.
